# Methodology

## Source

Official Knesset OData service: https://knesset.gov.il/OdataV4/ParliamentInfo/ . Generated by `scripts/scorecard.py`; anyone can rerun it and get the same numbers.

## Columns

- **Bills initiated** — private bills where the MK is listed as an initiator (`KNS_BillInitiator`, `IsInitiator = true`) in the previous Knesset. Co-signing counts the same as leading. Quantity says nothing about quality.
- **Parliamentary questions** — questions to ministers submitted by the MK (`KNS_Query`) in the previous Knesset.
- **Agenda motions** — motions for the agenda initiated by the MK (`KNS_Agenda`).
- **Plenum votes cast** — recorded votes by the MK (`KNS_PlenumVoteResult`) divided by all plenum votes held between the MK's first and last day in the previous Knesset. Limits: ministers and deputy ministers often vote less by role; opposition boycotts and coalition "pairing" agreements lower the number without meaning absence; a few votes are not recorded by name.
- **New in K26** — the MK was not a member in the previous Knesset; no history yet. The current Knesset was sworn in on 10.11.2026.
- **Our letters: sent / replied** — only letters in `letters/` with a real send date. A reply = any substantive answer from the MK or their office (an automatic acknowledgement does not count). We wait 30 days before counting a letter as unanswered.
- **Median reply days** — days from send to first substantive reply.

## Ministries table

For each parliamentary question in the previous Knesset: answered = the Knesset record has a minister reply date. Late = reply date minus the planned reply date in the record.

## Fairness rules

- Every MK gets letters of the same quality. When we measure responsiveness across all MKs, the letter is the same question, written individually, sent the same day.
- We publish the letters, so anyone can judge whether a question deserved an answer.
- Errors are corrected publicly in the git history.
