# Existing public data on how active MKs are

What already exists in Israel, checked 19.11.2026.

## Do MKs answer citizens' emails?

There are no current public numbers. The only tests found are from ynet:

| When | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | Email to every MK | 46 of 120 replied |
| Oct 2007 | 120 emails, 30+ days to reply, a reminder after 3 weeks | 36 of 119 replied (about 30%), all via aides; some mailboxes bounced |

Sources: [ynet 2007](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/1,7340,L-3457187,00.html), [ynet earlier test](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3040438,00.html). A local version (Rehovot city council): [mynet Rehovot](https://rehovot.mynet.co.il/local_news/article/m_371568).

No one has repeated this in almost 20 years, so our own reply-rate measurement fills that gap.

## Attendance and activity trackers

| Source | What it measures per MK | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Knesset Time (זמן כנסת) | Hours in the Knesset building, committee attendance (from protocols, a few weeks behind), vote attendance, questions, rankings, MK-to-MK comparison | [presence](https://zmanknesset.co.il/presence/mks), [committees](https://zmanknesset.co.il/committees/presence), [votes](https://zmanknesset.co.il/votes/presence), [rank](https://zmanknesset.co.il/rank), [compare](https://zmanknesset.co.il/comparison/mks) |
| Shakuf "Parlameter" | Weighted score for the whole 25th Knesset: committee attendance 35%, building attendance 30%, plenum votes 25%, parliamentary questions 10% | [25th Knesset summary](https://shakuf.co.il/64289), [how it is built](https://shakuf.co.il/31711) |
| Shomrim — 25th Knesset database | Legislation, speeches, parliamentary questions, motions | [Shomrim database](https://www.shomrim.news/collections/eng-knesset) |
| Israel Democracy Institute | Parliamentary work index by party (2018) | [IDI](https://www.idi.org.il/parliaments/20652/20676) |
| Knesset official data | Bills, questions, motions, plenum votes (what our scorecard uses) | [OData](https://knesset.gov.il/OdataV4/ParliamentInfo/) |
| Knesset Public Inquiries unit | 6,249 inquiries from the public to the Knesset in 2024 (about the Knesset as an institution, not per MK) | [2024 report (PDF)](https://m.knesset.gov.il/about/departments/Documents/PublicInquiriesReport2024.pdf) |

## Known findings from these sources

- Shakuf, 25th Knesset: Aryeh Deri attended zero committee sessions in the whole term (per Knesset Time data); top committee attendance was Yinon Azoulay, then Gilad Kariv and Simcha Rothman ([Shakuf](https://shakuf.co.il/64289)).
- Parliamentary questions (our scorecard, 25th Knesset): the Prime Minister's Office answered 22% of questions it got; Defense 47%; National Security 48%; Education 91% ([ministries.md](ministries.md)).

## Limits of all of these

Attendance is not quality or impact. Ministers cannot file private bills or questions. Committee chairs are counted as fully present. Opposition boycotts and "pairing" lower vote numbers without meaning absence.
