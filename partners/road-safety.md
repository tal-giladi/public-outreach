## The Road Safety Authority (הרלב"ד) — why it is weak

- Set up by law in 2006 after the Sheinin committee (2005), to lead national road safety.
- Budget: 550 million NIS planned in 2008 (only about half used), cut by 200 million in 2009, ~240 million in 2015, about 63 million in recent years, and cut another 10% to 62.5 million in 2024 — which cut traffic education and field work ([walla](https://cars.walla.co.il/item/3710849), [walla](https://cars.walla.co.il/item/1335863)). Numbers from press reports; verify against budget books.
- State Comptroller: special road-safety report, May 2024 ([full report](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2024/Road-Safety/2024-Road-Safety-001-HE.pdf)); earlier report on the Authority and its relations with the Transport Ministry, 2016 ([2016](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/67a/2016-67a-502-RashutLeumi.pdf)).
- Knesset Economic Committee discussed the Authority's status in Dec 2024 ([protocol](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/Committees/25_ptv_5585442.doc)).

Campaign angle: "The body that is supposed to stop the killing lost almost 90% of its budget." One clear, checkable ask: restore the Authority's budget and powers to the level the law intended, with a public yearly report. To do: read the 2024 Comptroller chapter and the Dec 2024 protocol before writing that email.

The Authority is also a partner, not only a target: its staff want the same thing. Ask for data and meetings, not only criticism.

## Bereaved families — rules

People who lost someone are the most powerful voice in this campaign, and the easiest to hurt. So:

1. **Only through organisations they already trust** (Yad Hanik'tafim, Or Yarok). We never contact a family we found in a news story.
2. **They decide everything**: whether, when, what is told, which name and photo, and they can withdraw at any time. Written consent, kept privately (not in this repo).
3. **No pressure on timing.** Never approach soon after a loss.
4. **Their words, not ours.** We do not write stories in their name and do not use a death as a subject line.
5. **Give back**: their demand (for example a state memorial day for road victims) becomes one of our asks, and we report every answer to them first.
6. Point families to support (Welfare Ministry centres, Yad Hanik'tafim) whether or not they join.
