# Road deaths

## Facts (verify against the original reports before sending)

- 2025: 455 killed in 434 fatal crashes (National Road Safety Authority); CBS counts 459 killed, the highest in 19 years. Source: [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/israel/article-1385951), [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/car/news/article/20025679).
- Breakdown 2025 (press reports of Authority data): 139 in private cars, 121 pedestrians, 101 motorcyclists (~22%), 34 children; 152 from Arab society, about double its share of the population. Source: [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/h1ncz4bgfg), [Kan](https://www.kan.org.il/content/kan-news/local/982850/).
- Government target: halve road deaths within a decade (national road safety plan). Source: [auto.co.il](https://www.auto.co.il/articles/car-news/industry/national-road-safety-plan/).
- Official trend report: [NRSA trends 2024 (PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/trends_2024/he/research_trends_2024_v03.pdf).

## Open questions for the FOI request (National Road Safety Authority + Ministry of Transport + Israel Police)

1. Enforcement cameras: number budgeted vs. installed vs. operating, per year 2022–2026.
2. The official list of dangerous road sections/junctions, with the planned and actual fix date for each.
3. Road-safety budget: approved vs. actually spent, per year 2022–2026.
4. Status of each measure in the national plan to halve deaths: target, owner, deadline, status.

## The asks

- To MKs: file a parliamentary question to the Transport Minister on items 1–4; ask the committee chair to hold a session once the relevant committee is formed (26th Knesset committees are still temporary as of 19.11.2026).
- To the committee: position paper with the FOI answers.
